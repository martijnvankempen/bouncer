# PEW vs BOUNCER

Pew the cat-octopus puts on boxing gloves and takes on a beefed-up bouncer in
a pink "Partner of the Year" jacket, Street Fighter style, to get on the list.

## Usage

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| LEFT / RIGHT (buttons or touch d-pad) | Walk back / forward; hold LEFT (back) to block |
| SELECT (button or big touch pad) | PUNCH: mash or hold for jab, cross, kick, uppercut; start, resume |
| RIGHT + SELECT | Lead with the kick |
| UP | Jump; PUNCH in the air for a dive kick |
| DOWN | PEW!: a star shot; with a full SUPER bar, the OCTO-RUSH super |
| MENU or BACK (touch pad) | Pause; BACK again returns to the title |
| HOME | Back to the badge menu |

Pressing SELECT on the title first shows a HOW TO PLAY card: a drawing of the
badge with labelled arrows pointing at each control. It stays up until you
press SELECT again to fight. Wherever the game asks for SELECT, a bouncing
arrow on the screen's edge points at the real button.

Each night is best of three 60-second rounds. Pew and the bouncer stand the
same height and both have full punch and kick animations. Hits fill Pew's
SUPER bar; when it's full Pew glows and DOWN fires the OCTO-RUSH: a cut-in
with Pew's face, then an 8-hit flurry ending in an uppercut.

The bouncer jabs, front-kicks, throws telegraphed haymakers, belly-bumps,
ground-pounds (jump the shockwave) and pops his jacket snaps at you. His RAGE
bar fills over time and when you hit him; when it's full he calls LAST CALL,
his own super: a red cut-in, a flex he can't be staggered out of, then a
shoulder charge. Hold back to block it and you get a big SUPER boost.

Win two rounds to get on the list: the jacket flies onto Pew and the next
night's bouncer is bigger. Lose and Pew gets thrown down the hallway, then a
6-second ad for the jacket that can't be skipped, then the name entry for a
top-five score or the title screen.

Jab, cross, kick and uppercut are worth 100, 150, 200 and 300; combos of 3+
hits add 25 x hits²; a K.O. adds 1000 per night, and winning a night adds
time, health and PERFECT bonuses.

Your best score, the top-five Hall of Fame and your initials are kept on the
badge. No Wi-Fi or extra hardware is needed.
